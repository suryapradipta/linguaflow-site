Good to know

Which videos can I use?

Any MP4, MOV or M4V video you have on your iPhone, from Photos or Files. LinguaFlow doesn't download from YouTube or other sites, and MKV files aren't supported.

Is it for beginners?

Yes. Every word shows pinyin and its HSK level, Kit explains sentences in plain language, and you choose your level when you start.

Which languages are translations in?

Subtitles can be translated into 13 languages, including English, Bahasa Indonesia, Spanish, Japanese and Korean.

Does it work offline?

Watching, the dictionary, saved words and review work offline. AI explanations and pronunciation feedback need a connection.

How do I cancel Pro?

Pro is billed by Apple. Cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.
